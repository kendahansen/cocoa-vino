Privacy

Last updated October 30, 2026

The short version

We collect as little as possible. This site sets no cookies, runs no ads or tracking, and loads nothing from other companies unless you ask for it (like the map). We never sell or share your information.

Newsletter

If you join our email list, your email address is stored with Kit (kit.com), the service we use to send the newsletter. We use it only to send you the newsletter. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time.

Maps

The map on our Visit page is provided by Google. It only loads if you choose to open it, at which point Google's own privacy policy applies.

Private event requests

If you ask about a private event, we use your name, email, phone and event details only to plan it with you.

Event tickets

Tickets and RSVPs are handled on our event pages. Anything you enter there is used only to manage your booking.
