# Cocoa Vino brand voice

Use this for every word on the site, in the newsletter and on social posts.

## Who we are
A high-end wine bar with a fire pit patio in Capitola Village, a short walk from the beach. Wine is the excuse, the night is the product.

**Positioning line:** Wine bar in the afternoon. Fire pit at sunset. Music at seven.

**What makes us different:** Any bottle on the wall can be poured by the glass, thanks to our Coravin. Champagne included. Most bars pour a short list by the glass; we pour the whole wall.

## Voice rules
- **Specific over adjective.** Every sentence should contain something you could photograph: the fire pit, the wine wall, the Rare bottles, the bingo cause.
- **Second person, present tense.** "Walk up from the beach. Grab a seat at the fire pit."
- **Short.** Hero is one line plus one supporting sentence. No paragraph longer than three sentences.
- **Name the nights.** Wednesdays bingo & trivia, Thursdays house DJ, Friday to Sunday live music, select Mondays paint parties & classes. Closed Monday and Tuesday.
- **Playful, never stiff.** "Wine needs a wingman." "Get the week's lineup before the village does."
- **Never use:** refined, inviting, sophistication, soulful, nestled, curated, elevated, savor the moment, designed to please every palate, for the seasoned collector, check back soon.
- **No em dashes. No italics. No small or light type.**

## Look
- **Colors:** velvet navy `#0a0f1f` / `#1b2650`, gold `#c9a35c` / `#e2c27e`, cream `#fffaf3`.
- **Type:** Bodoni Moda for headlines, Cinzel capitals for the wordmark, navigation, buttons and labels, Jost for body text.
- **Details:** a short gold bar under titles, a gold line under the header, squared buttons. No ornaments or flourishes.
- **Photos:** real photos of the space, wine and food. Wine or the patio in every shot. No stock, and no AI-generated people.
